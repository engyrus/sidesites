    Click a site to add it to your list. You can rename it or change its settings above afterward.

    Notes

    • The Desktop/Mobile button picks which version of a site you get. Mobile makes SideSites identify your browser to the site as Chrome on an Android phone, which often gives a layout better suited to the narrow side panel. The setting applies to every site on the same host name.
    • The Fit checkbox zooms a site down when its layout is wider than the side panel, and smoothly zooms it back up as you widen the side panel.
    • Sites in the side panel share the cookies of your normal browser profile. If signing in to a site inside the side panel doesn't work, sign in using a regular tab, then reload the site in the side panel. (Note: Gmail does not currently work in the side panel; we're working on it.)
    • To let sites load in the side panel, SideSites removes the headers that forbid framing — but only for pages loaded in the side panel. Pages in ordinary tabs are unaffected.
    • Pressing 1–9 in the SideSites menu opens the corresponding site. You can assign a shortcut to open the menu at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

    Saved pairs appear in the SideSites menu so you can open two sites in split view with one click. You can also save or remove pairs directly from the menu while in split mode. Changes are saved automatically.

      Notes

      • Clicking a pair in the SideSites menu opens both sites together in split view.
      • When split view is active with two saved sites that aren't already a pair, it appears at the top of the Pairs section in the menu with a + button to save it; saved pairs show a × button to remove them.
      • In split view, hover over a pane to focus it. Click the × button in its upper-right corner to close that pane and expand the other, or right-click / Ctrl+click × to expand the active pane to full height.
      • Drag the divider between panes to resize (or double-click it to reset to 50/50), and right-click the Split button in the menu header to swap the top and bottom panes without reloading.
